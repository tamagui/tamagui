# Privacy Policy

Tamagui.dev is a public site for Tamagui documentation, examples, and tools. The site has no user accounts, sign-in, or purchase flows, and does not store visitor profiles.

## Site preferences

The site stores display preferences, such as the selected theme and documentation syntax, in your browser's local storage or cookies. These preferences stay in your browser.

## Usage analytics

The site loads analytics scripts to understand aggregate site usage. Those providers may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser details, pages visited, and interaction events under their own privacy policies. The site does not associate analytics events with a signed-in account.

## Hosting and external links

Cloudflare delivers the site's static files and may process request data to operate and protect its network. Links to other websites are governed by those sites' privacy policies.

## Contact

For questions about this policy, contact support@tamagui.dev.
