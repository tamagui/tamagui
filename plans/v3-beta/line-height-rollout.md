- Core reads/writes `GetStyleResult.nativeTextMetrics` separately from host styles. Numbers in this record are ratios; px strings are lengths. `ComponentContext.parentFontSize` and `parentLineHeight` carry scalar inheritance. DOM primitives receive `__textMetrics` and strip it from host props. `StaticConfig.isDOM` identifies frames whose primitives own finalization.
